\subsection{Purpose and Scope}
\label{sec:purpose and scope}

A standard output from LOFAR2.0 calibration and imaging pipelines are FITS format images. These images will be in the standard FITS format as defined by \cite{FITSv3.0}\footnote{For the most recent definition, currently v4, see \url{https://fits.gsfc.nasa.gov/fits_standard.html}}. In addition to the standard FITS format, these data products will be accompanied by various ancillary data or "metadata" that are relevant to radio FITS images and specifically LOFAR2.0 FITS images. This Data Definition Document lays out the formal data specification for LOFAR2.0 FITS images.
